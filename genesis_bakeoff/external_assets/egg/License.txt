Note on shape/orientation (relevant to scenario_2b.py's use of this asset):
the raw mesh's local bounding box is x in [-0.2366, 0.2366], y in [-0.3476,
0.3081] (elongated axis), z in [-0.2366, 0.2366] -- i.e. the egg's long axis
is local Y and its two short (round, ~equal) axes are local X/Z. Loaded with
NO rotation (identity euler/quat), this places the elongated axis along
world Y (horizontal) and the round cross-section along world Z (vertical) --
i.e. the egg lies on its side by default, exactly the natural at-rest pose
of a bare egg placed on a flat table (no egg cup), matching the brief's
"a bare egg on the table is fine" note.
