## 1. Structural change: reorder Sec. III so E3 leads

Sec. III currently runs E2 -> verbalization -> E3, so the probe result leads and
re-prompting arrives third. The requested flow is *motivate re-prompting -> show
it fails -> explain why*, which is E3-first. Reorder to:

1. **Setup.** One paragraph: one checkpoint, seed-matched SAFE/UNSAFE twins.
2. **E3 motivation.** Re-prompting is the natural remedy: no retraining, no extra
   model, no change to the control stack. (Already written.)
3. **E3 result.** Table I (already added): per-suite baseline vs rewritten range,
   115 cells, none reducing hazard contact.
4. **Why it fails, part 1 -- the channel is redundant.** E2 probes: the hazard is
   already encoded at AUC 0.998 at the position the action head reads. Table II
   plus the new Fig. (Sec. 2 below). Naming it adds no information.
5. **Why it fails, part 2 -- the channel is inert.** 0% on ten perception controls;
   the text readout does not survive action fine-tuning.
6. **Implication.** The safety decision must be made outside the policy.

Net effect: E2 keeps all its content but becomes *subordinate* to E3, which is
what "focus on E3, keep the probes" means. No numbers change; this is a reorder
plus connective sentences.
