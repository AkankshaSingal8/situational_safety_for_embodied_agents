Round 2 · randomised scenes · every number

All results, randomised proof of concept

This view lists every result from round 2, the version with randomised scenes. It covers all three training seeds, then every condition for seed 0 on both the train and test scenes. The Report view summarises the same numbers. Tables are generated from runs_v2/seeds/seeds.md and runs_v2/results_v2.md and are not edited by hand.

How to read the tables

ScenesEach scene is randomised: mug start, target, laptop pose and size, carry height, and the base policy's tilt (35–60°, random axis and timing). There are 100 train scenes and 100 held-out test scenes. The base trajectory carries the mug over the laptop and tilts it in every scene.
A (laptop rule)Don't carry the mug over the laptop. A violation means the mug's centre is inside the laptop footprint, inflated by the mug's size, while the mug is held.
B (upright rule)Keep the mug upright. A violation means the mug tilts more than 15° while held.
successAfter release and a 1.2 s settle, the mug is within 5 cm of the target, tilted under 10°, and on the table.
"A ok & succ"Rule A respected and the task succeeded. "B ok & succ" and "both ok & succ" work the same way.
Modelsmain: the text-conditioned residual flow.
notext: the same model without text.
shuffled: text drawn independently of the target.
mean_A / mean_B: the average residual for each rule.
main_ab: main, also trained on combined A+B wordings.
Exp 2 / Exp 3 termsheldout and heldout2: two sets of 8 unseen wordings for each rule.
direct: the combined sentence fed as is.
compose: velocity composition.
stack / clauses in turn: one clause's correction, then the other's.
sum: both corrections added.
far set: combined sentences worded far from main_ab's training phrasings.
Brackets95% Wilson interval for the rate.
b / c, pPaired comparison on the same scenes: b counts scenes where the first arm wins, c where the second wins. p is the exact two-sided binomial (McNemar) test.
