Text-conditioned residual flow on SafeLIBERO

The same recipe as the proof of concept, moved to a real benchmark. A flow-matching policy learns the LIBERO-Spatial tasks from images. A residual flow then takes the camera images and a safety sentence such as “don't hit the moka pot” and corrects the policy's motion around the named obstacle. Its training targets come from a geometric detour that uses privileged simulator state. All numbers on this page come from experiments/safelibero_residual/; the sections after the summary are generated from its README.

Summary

On held-out episodes the safety sentence raises both collision avoidance and task success, and removing it removes the gain. The test uses official episodes 25–49, 200 episodes, compared episode by episode. These episodes were never used for training or for choosing a model, and each model was run on them once.

  • Text against no text, same data and architecture: CAR 61/6 (p=1.5e-12), safe & success 48/6, TSR 44/27 (p=0.057, borderline).
  • Two obstacles, sentence names one (n=177): named obstacle hit 0.44, the other 0.60. Within a scene, the other obstacle alone is hit 54 times and the named one alone 26 times (p=0.002). Base shows the opposite bias, and the no-text model shows none.
  • Unseen obstacle types (retrained without milk carton and red mug): CAR on those types goes from 0.067 to 0.107 (4/1, p=0.38). On seen types the same model gives 0.09 to 0.32 (31/2, p=1e-7). New wordings for known objects transfer; new object categories learned from four training types do not.

Caveats

  • The TSR gain over the no-text model is borderline on the main test. In the second training run, used for the unseen-object test, the no-text model has the higher TSR on seen types (0.61 against 0.49). The CAR gain holds in both runs.
  • The residual reads images and the safety sentence as text. The task instruction reaches it only through the base policy's task ID (10 tasks), not as text.
  • The residual gets about 55% of the teacher's CAR gain (0.45 against 0.745). Only one training seed for each model.
  • Adding obstacle types and measuring how unseen-type CAR scales with the number of training types needs more compute. The account had 0 of 480 SU left on November 2.
